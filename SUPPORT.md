# Getting help

Ask questions and report bugs through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/lolables/lola-mod-project-docs/issues).

- **Question or bug:** open an issue. Include the slash command you ran,
  your assistant (for example Claude Code), and your lola version
  (`lola --version`).
- **Vulnerability:** do not open an issue. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Before you ask, [Getting started](docs/usage/getting-started.md) covers
install, uninstall, and common usage.
